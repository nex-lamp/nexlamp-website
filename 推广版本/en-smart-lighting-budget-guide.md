## Where Your Money Goes: The 4 Cost Buckets

| Component | Budget % | What You're Paying For |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Light Fixtures | 40-50% | CRI ≥90, tunable white 2700K-6500K, lumen maintenance |
| LED Drivers | 15-25% | Flicker-free dimming, connection stability, long-term reliability |
| Control System | 20-25% | Zigbee hub, smart switches, sensors, scene panels |
| Installation | 10-15% | Wiring, pairing, scene programming |

**Key insight**: LED drivers account for only ~18% of budget but cause 80% of disconnection and flickering issues. Cheap drivers use low-grade capacitors — you'll regret it within 6 months.

## Three Real-World Budgets

### Entry-Level: ¥5,000-8,000 (~$700-1,100)
**For**: Renters, small apartments (≤60m²), partial smart lighting

- Living room: 6× Zigbee spotlights + 3m LED strip → ¥1,200
- Bedroom: 2× smart ceiling lights → ¥800
- Dining: Tunable white pendant → ¥500
- Control: Zigbee hub + 4 smart switches → ¥800
- Installation: DIY or electrician → ¥500-1,500

> Spend where you spend the most time. Don't smart-enable rooms you barely use.

### Mid-Range: ¥12,000-20,000 (~$1,700-2,800)
**For**: 3BR apartments (90-120m²), whole-home smart lighting

Full no-main-light design with recessed spotlights, magnetic track, and LED strips across living room, 3 bedrooms, kitchen, and bathrooms. Zigbee hub + 3 scene panels + 5 sensors.

> ⚠️ **Critical timing**: Plan light positions and wiring during the electrical rough-in phase. Adding strips after tiles are laid is nearly impossible.

### High-End: ¥20,000+ (~$2,800+)
DALI dimming + KNX bus for large homes and villas.

## The 5 Most Painful Mistakes

**1. No neutral wire in switch boxes ⚠️**
The most expensive mistake to fix. Smart switches need a neutral wire. Tell your electrician during rough-in — it costs nothing. Fixing it later means tearing walls open (¥2,000+).

**2. Using Wi-Fi-direct smart bulbs**
Home routers struggle with 20+ devices. Above 5 lights, switch to **Zigbee** — self-healing mesh, local execution, no router bottleneck.

**3. Confusing downlights with spotlights**
- Spotlights: 24°-36° beam angle, focused light for accent walls
- Downlights: 60°-120° beam, uniform illumination for hallways/kitchens

**4. Using 4000K everywhere**
- Living room: 3500-4000K (bright but warm)
- Bedroom: 2700-3000K (melatonin-friendly)
- Kitchen/study: 4000-5000K (high CRI, task lighting)

**5. Installing everything before testing**
Install 1-2 lights first, verify pairing stability and dimming smoothness, then go all-in.

---

## 3 Money-Saving Tips

1. **Install in phases**: You'll discover which rooms actually need smart lighting after moving in
2. **Solder LED strip joints, don't use clips**: Oxidation causes intermittent dark sections
3. **Place the hub in the center**: Signal drops ~50% through two walls
