
No-main-light interiors look great — when the driver is right. When it isn't, you get flicker, premature failures, and smart-home dropouts.

China's GB 30255-2026 standard (effective Sept 2027) just raised the bar:

→ Standby power: 1.5W → 0.3W (an 80% cut)
→ Efficacy tiers tightened across the board
→ Smart and tunable-white luminaires now in scope

After 11 years shipping smart LED drivers at NEXLAMP, six pitfalls show up in nearly every project that goes sideways:

1. Looking at wattage, not lm/W
2. "0-10V compatible" drivers that don't actually dim smoothly
3. Driver sized 1:1 with fixture (no 10% headroom)
4. Smart protocol without local execution → fails on WAN drop
5. Driver ripple causing yellow/blue edge fringing in deep-cup fixtures
6. Driver warranty buried in the fine print

Our specifier SOP now includes a 72-hour burn-in on 10% of every batch — it catches 80% of infant-mortality failures before they reach the site.
