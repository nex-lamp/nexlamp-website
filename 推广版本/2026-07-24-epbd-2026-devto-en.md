## EPBD Is No Longer a Recommendation

If you sell LED drivers, luminaires, or lighting controls into the EU, your customers are going to start asking one question over and over from May 2026: *"Is this product EPBD compliant?"*

EPBD is the Energy Performance of Buildings Directive. The recast version (2024) tightens the rules for new commercial buildings, and from 5 May 2026 the clause on automatic lighting control becomes mandatory for every new non-residential build in scope.

In plain language: a passive luminaire with a dumb constant-current driver is no longer enough. New commercial sites must demonstrate three things at handover — automatic on/off, daylight-linked dimming, and energy-use reporting to the BMS.

---

## The Three "Musts" in Plain English

1. **Automatic on/off by zone, schedule, or occupancy.** Presence detection, time schedules, or daylight triggers. Passive wall switches do not count.
2. **Daylight harvesting near windows.** Light sensors that dim fixtures based on natural light contribution.
3. **Energy monitoring tied to the BMS.** Sub-metering by zone, hourly data export, BACnet/Modbus or DALI-2 part-251 reporting.

The directive also references EN 15193 (energy performance of lighting) and EN 12464 (workplace lighting). Both are now treated as verification standards during commissioning.

---

## What This Changes for LED Driver and Luminaire Makers

This is where the supply chain feels the squeeze:

- **Drivers must accept control inputs.** At minimum a 0–10 V or DALI-2 receiver. Resistive dim only no longer covers EU new-build projects.
- **Standby power ceiling.** EN 62384 plus the EPBD Annex push standby draw below 0.5 W. Drivers that idle at 1–2 W will lose their CE mark in commercial-grade tenders.
- **Ripple current ≤ 10%.** The 2024 update of EN 62384 narrows the limit. Higher ripple = visible flicker = automatic failure at commissioning.
- **Sensor integration becomes product, not accessory.** Microwave, PIR, or light sensors must be designed in from BOM stage, not bolted on at install.
- **Commissioning tools.** Drivers need a re-commissioning path: DALI-2 DT-6/DT-8, NFC, or Bluetooth. The old "set once and forget" model is now contractually risky.

## Protocol Reality Check — Which Bus Wins?

| Protocol | Type | Best For | EPBD Fit | Cost |
|----------|------|----------|----------|------|
| **DALI-2** | Digital bus | Large commercial, office, hospital | ★★★★★ | Mid |
| **KNX** | Field bus | High-end hotel, airport, mixed-use | ★★★★★ | High |
| **0–10 V** | Analog | Retrofit, budget commercial | ★★★ | Low |
| **Casambi** | BLE mesh | Renovation, retail, hospitality | ★★★ | Mid |
| **Matter** | IP wireless | Small commercial, chain retail | ★★★ | Mid |

For new builds aiming for the EPBD five-star label, DALI-2 + KNX dual-bus is the safest combo. For renovation work where pulling new cable is unrealistic, Casambi or Matter over wireless is the pragmatic answer.

## A Three-Phase Roadmap for Manufacturers

**Short term (2026 Q3–Q4)**

- Audit every EU-bound driver SKU and tag it with the supported control interfaces.
- Add a 0–10 V receiver board to legacy CC drivers — the BOM cost is cents, the price uplift is real.
- Begin DALI-2 certification through DiiA (3–6 months typical).
- Update datasheets to state *"EPBD 2026/5 compliant"*.

**Medium term (2027)**

- Expose BACnet/Modbus gateways so luminaires can hand off energy data to the BMS.
- Ship commissioning tooling: NFC programmers, Bluetooth setup apps, scene templates.
- Move from selling components to selling sub-systems.

**Long term (2028+)**

- Bundle light + driver + control into a "luminaire sub-station" deliverable for EPCs.
- Add tunable white, circadian presets, occupancy analytics.
- Align with the EU Net-Zero pathway for 2030.

## The Bottom Line

EPBD 2026/5 is not "another EU regulation". It redraws who wins EU commercial lighting tenders over the next decade. Vendors who can deliver a verified, certified, BMS-interoperable lighting sub-system will own the projects. Vendors still selling single-function components will quietly lose them.

If you are a manufacturer or brand-owner and you have not started DALI-2 certification yet, the cheapest move you can make this quarter is to add a 0–10 V interface to your top three SKUs. That alone keeps you in the conversation until the full DALI-2 audit lands.
