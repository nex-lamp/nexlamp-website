## The Three Rules of Rental Smart Lighting

1. **Never touch the original wiring** — no wall cutting, no switch replacement
2. **Reversible installation only** — 3M adhesive, magnetic mounts, paste-on rails
3. **Fully removable in 10 minutes** — landlord sees no modifications

## The 3-Hour Room-by-Room Setup

### Living Room (15 min)
1. Replace ceiling bulb with Zigbee smart bulb (¥40, Mi Home or Tuya)
2. Paste battery LED strip behind sofa
3. Plug smart plug behind TV cabinet for ambient floor lamp
4. Mount wireless 2-button switch by the front door

Result: Walk in → main light ramps to 50% warm + sofa strip glows in 3 seconds.

### Bedroom (20 min)
1. Main light: 2700K tunable-white smart bulb
2. Bedside: battery motion-sensor night light
3. Bed base: RGB strip at 1% brightness for midnight navigation
4. Bedside: wireless rotary knob for brightness control

### Kitchen/Bathroom (10 min)

## 5 Pitfalls That Will Cost You Money

### Pitfall 1: Bought a Smart Switch That Needs Neutral Wire
99% of Chinese rental apartments have **single-live-wire** circuits. You need either:
- Single-live-wire smart switch, or
- Battery wireless switch (recommended, no circuit work)

### Pitfall 2: All-Wi-Fi Bulbs Killed Your Router
10+ Wi-Fi bulbs sharing 2.4GHz bandwidth creates congestion. Use **Zigbee or Bluetooth Mesh** with one gateway. Router load drops 50%.

### Pitfall 3: Battery Lights With Non-Replaceable Cells
Cheap battery lights die in 1-2 years. Choose **18650 or AA replaceable battery** models only.

### Pitfall 4: 3M Adhesive Failing Mid-Night
Always:
- Clean surface with **alcohol wipe first**
- Wait **30 seconds** for adhesive to set
- For items >500g, add **screws or magnetic mount**

### Pitfall 5: Mixing Multiple Ecosystems
Mi Home, Tuya, HomeKit, Aqara, Yeelight don't talk to each other. **Pick ONE ecosystem** (I recommend Mi Home or Tuya for cost and openness).

## Moving Out: 10-15 Minutes, Zero Damage

1. **Smart bulbs**: Unscrew, take with you (leave regular bulb for landlord)
2. **Battery strips**: Heat 3M adhesive 30 seconds with hair dryer, peels off cleanly
3. **Smart plugs**: Unplug, replace with original outlet
4. **Wireless switches**: Battery models lift off; adhesive models heat and peel

All automations live in the cloud, so when you move to your new place, **everything still works**.
