# Understanding China's GB 30255-2026: The New Mandatory LED Lighting Standard

## Executive Summary

Effective March 1, 2026, China's **GB 30255-2026** standard mandates strict energy efficiency requirements for all indoor LED lighting products. For global lighting manufacturers and distributors targeting the Chinese market, understanding these changes is critical for product compliance and competitive positioning.

## Key Changes from the 2019 Standard

| Aspect | GB 30255-2019 | GB 30255-2026 |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Product Coverage** | Directional downlights only | + Non-directional bulbs, tubes, high-bay, retrofit lamps |
| **Min. Efficacy (Tier 3)** | Lower baseline | +15-30% higher threshold |
| **Mandatory Parameters** | Basic efficacy rating | + CRI, chromaticity tolerance, standby power |

## Efficiency Tier System

The standard establishes three tiers — products below Tier 3 cannot be manufactured or sold:

- **Tier 1** (Best-in-class): ≥120 lm/W for downlights
- **Tier 2** (Mid-range): ≥100 lm/W for downlights
- **Tier 3** (Market entry): ≥85 lm/W for downlights

## Implications for LED Driver Selection

The efficiency tier a fixture achieves is directly tied to its LED driver power supply quality. Key factors:

1. **Conversion efficiency** — Drivers with >93% efficiency enable Tier 1 ratings
2. **Thermal performance** — Stable output at 85°C+ prevents efficacy degradation
3. **Current regulation** — Tight ±3% current tolerance maintains consistent light output
